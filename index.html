THE PROJECT

A shared map.
A clearer store.

Finding a product shouldn’t depend on finding the right person. ShelfWise brings store layouts and product information together, helping shoppers find their way and staff keep everyone on the same page.

Managers create the layout. Employees maintain the details. Shoppers explore the same visual guide—with product locations, availability, pricing, and promotions in context.

Built by Will Black, Conner Francis, Bradley Lund, and Yi Yang for the University of Utah’s senior capstone.
